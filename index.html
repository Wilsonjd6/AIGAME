Corner Blocks

A Blokus-style game for 2 to 4 players on one screen. Your pieces must touch your own pieces at corners only, never along an edge. Your first piece must cover your corner. Drag a piece onto the board, then tap Place piece.
How to play

Goal: place as many of your 21 pieces as you can. The player with the most squares placed wins.

Placing: drag a piece from the tray onto the board. A green outline means the spot is legal and red means it isn’t. The outline stays where you drop it, so you can use Rotate and Flip, or drag again to adjust. Then tap Place piece (or press Enter). On a computer you can also just hover and click.

Rules: your first piece must cover your colored corner ring. After that, each piece must touch one of your own pieces at a corner, and never along an edge. Touching other colors is fine.

Stuck? If you have no legal moves, you’ll be asked to forfeit. CPU players move on their own.
